Install PokyPlane to play

On phones and tablets, PokyPlane only runs as an installed app (PWA) in landscape. Playing in the mobile browser is disabled.

  1. Open this site in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone)
  2. Use the browser menu → Install app / Add to Home Screen
  3. Open PokyPlane from your home screen

After installing, launch the app from your home screen.
